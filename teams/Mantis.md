
Team Mantis Introduction
Our team is responsible for designing and implementing the Record and Achievement System for the Space Invaders game.

The goal is to allow players to:
- Keep track of their scores and gameplay records.
- Earn achievements for reaching specific milestones.
- View previously earned achievements and high scores.
- Make the game more engaging by rewarding player progress.

| Achievement | Condition |
|---|---|
| **First Contact** | Score 1,000 points |
| **Space Fighter** | Score 5,000 points |
| **Galaxy Defender** | Score 10,000 points |
| **Untouchable** | Score 1,000 points without being hit |
| **Invincible** | Score 10,000 points without being hit |
| **Coin Collector** | Collect 1,000 coins |
| **New Record** | Beat your previous high score |
